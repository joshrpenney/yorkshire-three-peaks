16-week preparation programme

Build up gradually. Arrive ready.

Weekly structure: 2–3 easy aerobic sessions, 2 strength sessions, 1 long weekend hike and 1 recovery day. Move days around work and family commitments.

The priority is consistency and time on your feet. Most aerobic work should feel comfortable enough to hold a conversation.

🟢 Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4 — Build the base

W1: 2×30 min easy walk/run · 60–75 min long walk · 2× strength.

W2: 2×35 min easy walk/run · 90 min long walk · 2× strength.

W3: 2×40 min aerobic · 2 hr hilly walk · 2× strength.

W4: 2×40–45 min aerobic · 2.5 hr hilly walk · introduce a light backpack.

🔵 Phase 2 · Weeks 5–8 — Build endurance & hills

W5: 2×45 min aerobic · 3 hr hilly walk · 2× strength.

W6: 2×45–50 min aerobic · 3.5 hr hilly walk · 2× strength.

W7: 2×50 min aerobic · 4 hr hilly hike with sustained climbs · 2× strength.

W8: 1 easy aerobic session · 1 hill session · 4.5 hr hike. Practise food and hydration.

🟠 Phase 3 · Weeks 9–12 — Three Peaks specific

W9: 2 easy aerobic sessions · 5 hr hilly hike · 2× strength.

W10: 2 aerobic sessions · 5.5 hr hilly hike with climbing · 2× strength.

W11: 2 aerobic sessions · 6 hr hike. Test boots, socks, backpack, food and waterproofs.

W12: back-to-back weekend: 3 hr Saturday + 2 hr easy Sunday. Keep strength moderate.

🔴 Phase 4 · Weeks 13–16 — Peak preparation & taper

W13: 2 aerobic sessions · 6–6.5 hr hilly hike with full kit · 2 light strength sessions.

W14: 2 easy aerobic sessions · 7 hr challenge-style hike. Practise exact fuelling.

W15: reduce volume 30–40% · 3.5–4 hr easy hike · 2 short aerobic sessions · light strength.

W16: taper: 2×20–30 min easy walks, mobility and rest. No hard training in final 3 days.

Strength · twice per week

Session A

Goblet squat · Romanian deadlift · step-ups · calf raises · plank.

Session B

Split squat · hip hinge · reverse lunges · calf raises · side plank/dead bug.

Use 2–3 sets of 8–12 controlled reps. Keep 1–3 good reps in reserve rather than training to failure.

Important
  • Build duration gradually; don't suddenly double your walking.
  • Use hills, stairs or treadmill incline to develop climbing ability.
  • From Week 8, use the backpack you expect to take on the event.
  • By Week 11, test your exact boots, socks, food and clothing.
  • The goal is to arrive fit and fresh, not exhausted from training.
