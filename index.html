Training

8-week build-up

Weeks 1–2 · Get moving

2 × 30–45 min weekday walks + 1 × 1–1.5 hour weekend walk.

Weeks 3–4 · Time & hills

2 × 45 min walks + 1 × 2–2.5 hour weekend walk. Add hills and start carrying your backpack.

Weeks 5–6 · Endurance

2 × 45–60 min walks + 1 × 3–4 hour weekend walk. Practise food, water and uneven ground.

Week 7 · Rehearsal

Easy walks plus one 5–6 hour long walk using your event kit.

Week 8 · Taper

Two easy 30-minute walks. Prioritise recovery.
